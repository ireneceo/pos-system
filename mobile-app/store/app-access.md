# App access — 심사자 안내 (Play Console › App content › App access)

선택: «All or some functionality is restricted» → 아래 안내 1건 추가.

- Name: Demo restaurant (no password)
- Username / Password: 비움 (비밀번호 없이 들어가는 데모 카드)
- Any other information to access your app:

The app opens on the PurpleHere sign-in screen. Below the sign-in form there are "Demo account" cards.
Tap the "Restaurant" demo card to enter a demo restaurant without a password — no real customers or payments.
From there: POS (take an order), Floor plan, Live orders, Kitchen display and Settings › Printers.
Demo data is reset every night (GMT+8).
Printing needs a physical Bluetooth or network thermal printer; without one, print buttons show "no printer configured".

> 확인할 것(Opus, 빌드 뒤): 운영 purplehere.com/pos 로그인 화면에 데모 카드가 실제로 보이는지 — 화면 문구와 위 안내가 같아야 한다.
